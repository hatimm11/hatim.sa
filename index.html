Our story

Fewer things,
made properly.

HandmadeSmall batchMade to orderMade to last

Hatim started from a simple frustration: most everyday goods are made fast, look alike, and barely last. We wanted the opposite — a small studio making a few well-considered pieces by hand, in leather and woven palm, with patience instead of shortcuts.

Founded by Hatim, an illustrator and designer in Saudi Arabia, the studio treats every piece as something meant to be carried for years.

بدأت حاتم من ملاحظة بسيطة: أغلب المنتجات اليومية تُصنع بسرعة وتتشابه ولا تدوم. أردنا العكس — استوديو صغير يصنع قطعاً قليلة متقنة باليد، في الجلد والسعف، بصبرٍ لا باختصار.

02

Why it matters

Make less,
make it better,
make it last.

So much is produced quickly and discarded just as fast. We'd rather make fewer things and make them properly — pieces that earn their place and stay there.

يُنتَج الكثير بسرعة ويُرمى بسرعة. نحن نفضّل أن نصنع أقل ونتقنه — قطعٌ تستحق مكانها وتبقى.

  • →Made in small, deliberate runs.
  • →Responsible choices at every step we can control.
  • →Built to be kept, repaired, and carried for years.
  • →Less waste, by design — quality over volume.
  • →An eye on impact, from first sketch to finished piece.
