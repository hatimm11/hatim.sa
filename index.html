About

Education, technology, and research.

I've worked at the Ministry of Education for nine years. I design and build EdTech products, most notably Shawahid, a free platform for teachers in Saudi Arabia.

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Educational Administration and Supervision at King Khalid University, researching the use of agentic AI in education administration. I hold a Professional Master's in E-Learning and a Bachelor's in Business Administration.

Research

Doctoral dissertation.

Requirements for Employing Agentic AI to Advance Administrative Work across General Education Departments in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

The study examines the requirements for using agentic AI, systems that can plan and carry out tasks on their own, in the administrative work of Saudi education departments. It covers six dimensions:

  • Leadership
  • Organizational
  • Human
  • Technical
  • Ethical
  • Legislative
King Khalid University, College of Education. In progress.
