About

Teaching, research, and building — in one practice.

I teach financial literacy, vocational and career education, and managerial skills at Yanbu High School for Boys, with nine years at the Ministry of Education. I'm a PhD candidate in Educational Administration & Supervision at King Khalid University, researching the adoption of agentic AI in public-education administration.

I hold a Professional Master's in E-Learning and a Bachelor's in Business Administration. Alongside teaching, I design and build EdTech products — pairing instructional design with full-stack web and iOS development, and privacy-conscious use of generative AI.
