About

Building from inside the system.

For nine years I've worked at the Ministry of Education, close to the everyday realities of schools. That vantage point shapes the questions I research and the products I build.

I design and build EdTech products like Shawahid, and I'm a Ph.D. candidate at King Khalid University researching what it takes to employ agentic AI in education administration. I hold a Professional Master's in E-Learning, a Bachelor's in Business Administration, and three advanced professional certificates from the National eLearning Center.

Research

What it takes to put agentic AI to work in education.

Doctoral dissertation

Requirements for Employing Agentic AI to Advance Administrative Work across General Education Departments in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

Agentic AI goes beyond answering questions: it can plan and carry out multi-step tasks on its own. My study identifies what Saudi education departments need to employ it responsibly in administrative work. Grounded in organizational-readiness research, it examines requirements across six dimensions, to help decision-makers adopt it with confidence.

  • Leadership
  • Organizational
  • Human
  • Technical
  • Ethical
  • Legislative
